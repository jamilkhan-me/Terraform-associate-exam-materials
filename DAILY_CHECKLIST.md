# Daily Checklist

Follow this routine every day and record the outcomes in PROGRESS.md and SCORECARD.md yourself.

## Start of session (15–20 min)
- [ ] Open `PROGRESS.md` — read "Next recommended action"
- [ ] **Closed-book recall test**: re-answer yesterday's section J (Active recall) without looking, plus 10 cards from your weak topics
- [ ] Re-test any `mistakes/error-log.md` item marked "open"
- [ ] Review 15 cards from `flashcards/weak-topics.md`

## Learning block
- [ ] Read the day's lesson section by section
- [ ] For every **Active recall** prompt: answer out loud or in writing **before** expanding `<details>`
- [ ] For every **Predict** prompt: commit to an answer before reading on
- [ ] Note anything that surprised you — surprises are future exam traps

## Practice block
- [ ] Complete the day's lab(s) from `starter/` — use hints progressively (Hint 1 before Hint 2)
- [ ] Compare with `SOLUTION.md` only after finishing or after 20 min stuck
- [ ] Write one sentence: "The concept this lab proved is …"

## Assessment block
- [ ] Take the lesson's mastery quiz (section L) without notes, writing answers before opening them
- [ ] Give a confidence (1/2/3) per answer
- [ ] Read every explanation, including for questions you got right
- [ ] Mastery decision recorded: ✅ mastered · 🔁 re-teach · ➕ more practice

## Close of session (5 min)
- [ ] PROGRESS.md, SCORECARD.md, error-log, weak-topics updated
- [ ] Tomorrow's first action is written in PROGRESS.md

## Exam-technique habits to build every day
1. Read the **last line** of the question first: "select TWO", "which is NOT", "safest".
2. Identify the **object** being asked about: configuration, state, plan, or real infrastructure.
3. Eliminate options that are true statements but don't answer the question.
4. Watch absolute words: *always, never, only, all, guarantees*.
5. Don't fight the question with edge cases it didn't mention — answer the standard behaviour.
